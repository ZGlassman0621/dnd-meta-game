# Report — repo-check-fixes-spells: the spell catalog matches the book

## Understanding

This project is a solo D&D 2024 game: one human player, with an AI Dungeon Master (gemma4:12b, running locally) playing every NPC, companion and enemy. The engine owns the rules. It knows each spell's level, school, classes, range, duration, save and dice, it rolls the dice for companions and enemies, and it tells the player what to roll for their own character.

This job brings the engine's spell catalog in line with the 2024 Player's Handbook wherever the 2026-11-24 repo check found it wrong:

- one missing spell (Tsunami);
- wrong source tags, schools, class lists, casting times, ranges, components, durations and one ritual flag;
- invented, missing or flattened saves;
- wrong or missing damage dice;
- descriptions that state the wrong numbers.

It also asks for something the catalog never had: costly and consumed material components, stored and enforced, so Revivify can't be cast without its diamonds. Finally, the two copies of the catalog the client reads (the generated mirror and the old legacy files) must match the corrected catalog.

**One difference from the brief, stated first.** The brief points to six book-transcription files and the repo-check report, all in `~/workspace/dnd-app-review/` in Muse's workspace. None of them is on this PC: I searched `C:\Users\Zachd` and `E:\`, and the repo has no material text anywhere either. Every book value in this build therefore comes from the brief's own quotes. That was enough for items 1–8 and 10–11. It was not enough for item 9's data: the brief quotes material specifics for only 5 spells, and the book's Components lines for the other 209 PHB material spells aren't available. I built the field, the cast-time check, and the 5 quoted spells, and listed the rest as a gap. I did not fill them in from memory (handoff §4: never invent data).

**Step 0 check.** Every live value the brief quotes as "catalog: …" matched the PC copy on 2026-11-25 before any edit. The line numbers match too: `"banishing smite"` is at line 56, and its damage entry at line 620. No drift.

## Verdict

## What was built (in play)

- **A druid can now cast Tsunami**, at 8th level, from up to a mile away. The first wave deals 6d10 bludgeoning, half on a Strength save.
- **Spell lists are the book's.** A paladin finds Blinding Smite. Cleric, sorcerer and wizard find Sunbeam. Bards find Prismatic Wall, Slow, Eyebite and Antipathy/Sympathy. Magic Jar and Wall of Ice are wizard-only again. In all, 23 spells regained a book class and 12 lost a class the book doesn't give them. Homebrew classes (Artificer, Blackguard, Spellshot) stay where they were.
- **Spell cards read the book's facts**: school, casting time, range, components, duration, concentration and ritual for every spell the check listed. Banishing Smite and Forcecage now need concentration. Animal Shapes and the Blinding, Searing and Wrathful Smites no longer do. Plant Growth shows both its casting times.
- **No invented saves.** Hallow, Maze, Plane Shift and Ray of Sickness no longer force a save the book doesn't have. Plane Shift is no longer an attack spell. Forcecage and Magic Circle record their Charisma save as happening *only if* a creature tries to teleport out. Bigby's Hand, Prismatic Wall and Symbol list every save the book gives them.
- **The engine rolls the book's dice:**
  - Inflict Wounds is 2d10.
  - Ice Storm is 2d10 + 4d6.
  - Cordon of Arrows is 2d4.
  - Prismatic Spray is 12d6.
  - Vitriolic Sphere is 10d4, plus the later 5d4.
  - Witch Bolt opens with 2d12.
  - Faithful Hound is a Dexterity save for 4d8 force.

  Meteor Swarm, Jallarzi's Storm of Radiance, Ice Storm, Flame Strike and Destructive Wave now record their damage types separately. When a companion casts one, a fire-resistant enemy halves only the fire part. When a player casts a multi-dice spell, the roll prompt names every die ("2d10+4d6"); before, it named only the first.
- **Costly material components are enforced.** With fewer than 300 GP of diamonds in the party's pack, Revivify is refused before any slot is spent, and the refusal says what's missing. With enough diamonds, the cast spends the cheapest diamonds that cover the cost and keeps the rest. Heroes' Feast likewise needs and consumes its bowl. Astral Projection needs a 1,000 GP jacinth, Arcane Lock 25 GP of gold dust, and Swift Quiver a quiver. Spells with ordinary components (Fireball) are unaffected: a pouch or focus covers them.
- **Every spell surface agrees.** The character sheet, quick reference, party builder, level-up page and the early creator rows used to show 2014 text for Blade Ward, Guidance, Resistance and others. They now show the same text as the live catalog.

## Decisions made (reversible, for veto)

1. **Multi-save shape.** `save` stays the single ability the engine rolls on cast. A new `saves` list holds every book save as `{ ability, when, conditional? }`, read through `spellSaveList()`. *Reversal:* delete the `saves` fields and the helper; nothing else reads them.
2. **Conditional saves.** Forcecage and Magic Circle have no `save` field. Their Charisma save sits in `saves` with `conditional: true` and the trigger, and the description carries it too. *Reversal:* data-only.
3. **Per-type damage shape.** A `damageParts` list holds `[{ damage, damageType }]`, and the existing `damage` stays the total. Upcast dice are shared evenly across same-size parts (Jallarzi's +1d10 each, matching its own description). Companion casts apply resistance per part. *Reversal:* remove `damageParts` and the companion branch in `spellCastService.js`.
4. **Multi-round shape.** A `followUps` list holds `[{ when | round, damage | healing, damageType, save?, … }]`. It is data only; not wired. *Reversal:* data-only.
5. **Conditional damage.** Entries with a `condition` field return nothing from `spellEffectSpec` unless the caller asks (`includeConditional`). This keeps Earthquake's 12d6 from landing on every failed Dexterity save. *Reversal:* one line in `spellCatalog.js`.
6. **Plant Growth's two modes.** `castTime` is "1 action or 8 hours", so the turn economy treats it as an action, plus a `castModes` list. *Reversal:* data-only.
7. **Material field + check.** The field is `material: { text, costGp, consumed, match }`. The check runs before any slot is spent: on a player cast, companion cast, readied hold and party cast. Consumed parts are spent after a successful cast. The check reads the party inventory, which lives on the recruiting character. An item's value comes from its own price, a "(N gp)" in its name, or the exact book price. Unpriced items don't count. The cheapest covering set is spent. A readied release, and a Wild Magic random-spell cast, skip the check: the hold already paid, and the surge casts for you. *Reversal:* remove `spellMaterialService.js` and its five call sites. Known edge: two 500 GP bowls satisfy "a bowl worth 1,000+ GP".
8. **`consumed: null`** for Astral Projection and Arcane Lock until the book line is known, so the engine doesn't spend them. *Reversal:* set to true.
9. **Ice Storm's upcast die is 1d8 → 1d10.** The old 1d8 matched the old wrong 2d8; left alone, it would have silently stopped scaling. Not stated in the brief. *Reversal:* one value.
10. **Faithful Hound: no damage on a successful save**, from the brief's "Dexterity save or 4d8 Force damage". *Reversal:* one value.
11. **Control Water's `save: "str"` is unconditional.** The engine rolls it only when the cast names a target, which in practice is the whirlpool. *Reversal:* data-only.
12. **Legacy files: option (a)**, content replaced from the live catalog by a re-runnable script, with a test pinning them. I chose this over removing the reads because it leaves five UI components untouched. *Reversal:* `git revert` the data files.
