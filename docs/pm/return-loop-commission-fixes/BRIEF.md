# Brief — return-loop-commission-fixes: commission deadlines are read on the scale they were set on

## Goal (in play)
When you order something to be made and come back later, it becomes ready when its making time is actually up — not early just because the campaign is more than a year old — and it stays ready for its collection window, measured the same way its deadline was set.

## Background

This job fixes the commission gap only — 2 assertions. The downtime and scene-evolution gaps stay red, exactly as named in that report, pending Zach's scope call.
- Gap — the session-end sweep reads commission deadlines on the wrong day scale. The sweep passes the clock's **absolute** day counter (`server/services/sessionEndService.js:109`, `getCurrentDay`) to `processDueOrders` and `expireStaleReadyOrders` (`server/services/deferredResolutionSweep.js:48, 70`). Commission deadlines are set on the **day-of-year** scale (`server/services/merchantOrderService.js:429`, `currentGameDay: character.game_day`), and the living-world tick correctly uses that scale (`server/services/livingWorldService.js:226`). The two scales are equal only until the first new year; after that, the absolute counter is past every deadline. Effect: once a campaign is more than a year old, every pending commission comes due at the next session end, however far off its deadline is, and it is stamped ready on an absolute-scale day. The 30-day expiry then compares two different scales as well. Only legacy rows are affected today, because placing new commissions is blocked while `DEFERRED_RESOLUTION_ACTIONS` is off. Failing assertions: `[tick] GAP G-3: the commission is stamped ready on the day-of-year scale its deadline uses, day 103 (got 468 — the absolute day counter)` and `[tick] GAP G-3: the commission not due until day 150 is still being made (got ready …)`.
- Surveyed call chain (from the return-loop report, live bytes at `ab52d37` — verify against live bytes before trusting): sweep stage is `getCurrentDay` (the absolute day counter), then `sweepDeferredResolutions` (companion activities, due commissions, stale commissions) at `sessionEndService.js:104-118`, `server/services/deferredResolutionSweep.js:33-91`. The return-loop fixture is a campaign one year in (absolute day 465 = day 100 of 1492), which is what makes this gap visible; on a first-year clock these assertions would pass by coincidence. Both scales are kept in sync in the fixture, as `scheduleService` requires.
- Settled decisions that shape this job (do not re-decide): the 9 return-loop gap checks stay real, failing assertions and are never edited to pass (PM decision, Muse, 2026-11-25, confirmed at the gate 2026-11-26) — a gap check turns green only because the product fix makes it pass; testing (Zach, 2026-11-24): no browser test per job, one test once everything is done, per-job gate is Zach's full `npm test` only; the PC is the only working copy (Zach, 2026-11-13); one job at a time — this job runs after the experience fixes land, on the latest `phase-4-hybrid-combat`.
