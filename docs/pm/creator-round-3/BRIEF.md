# Brief — creator-round-3: creator retest fixes (picker layout, missing descriptions, two copy fixes)

## Goal (in play)
When a player walks through the creator, every picker reads cleanly and every choice explains itself. Picking cantrips, class skills, or starting equipment shows each name beside its checkbox — not a checkbox in one column and a name stranded at the far edge, wrapping mid-word. A player who sees "Mark of Warding" under race variants knows what a mark is. A dwarf picking artisan tools under Clan's Craft knows what each tool is for. Alignment, Faith, and Lifestyle choices each say what they mean. And the Story & Appearance page speaks to the player, not about the system.
