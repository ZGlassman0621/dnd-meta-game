The life that shaped him

❧

Raised in the Wraithwood hermitage by Master Yrre — a wandering monk who took in the children no village would — Dravyr learned the Open Hand, long silences, and how to listen to a forest until it told him its secrets.

The winter Yrre walked into the trees and did not return, Dravyr found what his master had hidden: a discovery the orders would kill to bury. He has carried it down out of the high passes ever since — travelling light, owing debts, and stopping, against his better judgement, wherever the hungry remind him of the boy he used to be.
