Your origin, as the world reads it

I was raised in the Wraithwood hermitage, a moss-eaten cloister three days from the nearest road, by Master Yrre — a wandering monk who took in the children no village would. He taught me the Open Hand, and silence, and how to listen to a forest until it told you its secrets.

One winter Yrre walked out to meditate at the tree-line and did not come back. In looking for him I found what he had been hiding: a discovery in his cell that the orders would kill to bury. I have carried it ever since.

I owe a debt, too, to the monastery of the high passes who sheltered me after — and to my sister, Ledra, whom I left at the tree-line and have not seen since.
